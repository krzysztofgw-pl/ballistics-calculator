Ballistics is a free long range ballistic calculator for precision rifle shooters and hunters. Enter your rifle, scope and load once, and it tells you what to dial on the turrets or where to hold on your reticle, at any distance. No ads, no tracking, works offline.

WHAT TO DIAL OR HOLD
• Elevation and windage in MRAD, MOA or SMOA (shooter's MOA), rounded to whole turret clicks
• Turret correction from a tall-target test, for scopes that don't track true
• Both turrets drawn around your reticle: turn them by hand, reset them with one tap
• The holds drawn on your scope's reticle (DLR-3, DLS-1, DLS-3, Mil-Dot, tree, hash and duplex in MRAD or MOA), first or second focal plane, at any magnification
• Choose per reticle whether you dial or hold elevation and windage
• Wind flag in the scope view showing the wind's strength and direction
• Moving targets: one tap, set the speed and course, and the lead hold appears on the reticle

DOPE CARD AND RANGE CARD
• DOPE chart to your maximum range: drop, elevation, clicks, wind drift, velocity, energy, time of flight and Mach
• Range card with saved targets: distance, angle, azimuth and size
• Charts of wind drift by wind speed and direction, spin drift by distance, hit probability by distance and Coriolis by azimuth

WIND AND WEATHER
• Wind dial: a wind clock inside a compass ring that turns with your shot azimuth
• Crosswind and head or tailwind shown under the dial
• Phone compass with declination and calibration check
• Wind forecast for your location (Open-Meteo): a 12-hour chart of mean wind, gusts and direction; drag the window over your shooting hours
• Shooting height correction (10 m, open ground, woods)
• Temperature, humidity and pressure from the forecast
• Pressure from your phone's barometer or from a Garmin watch with the free Station Pressure app, with a Test button
• Altitude and latitude in one tap, from a terrain map or GPS
• Shot angle (incline) measured with the phone camera

CHRONOGRAPH IMPORT
• Labradar, Labradar LX, Garmin Xero C1 (CSV or FIT), Athlon Rangecraft, MagnetoSpeed V3 and Sporter, or any table with velocities
• Or type your velocities shot by shot, in m/s or fps
• Average, SD and ES, misread shots flagged
• Save the muzzle velocity for a rifle or a load, corrected for powder temperature

ACCURATE BALLISTICS SOLVER
• 3-DOF point-mass trajectory with G1, G7, G2, G5, G6, G8, GI, GS and RA4 drag models
• Custom drag curves Cd(Mach) from Doppler radar data and velocity-banded BCs
• Humid-air atmosphere, density altitude, station pressure or QNH
• Coriolis and Eötvös effects, spin drift, aerodynamic jump and Miller stability, each switchable
• Powder temperature sensitivity and separate zero conditions
• Cross-checked against an independent open-source ballistics engine

YOUR RIFLES AND LOADS
• Profiles for rifles, scopes and loads, with a sight height helper
• Muzzle velocity per rifle, and the barrel length of the factory test
• Suppressor on or off, with its point of impact shift
• Library of over 600 bullets from Barnes, Berger, Hornady, Lapua, Sierra and Speer with G1 and G7 BCs, plus factory loads
• BC truing from a measured drop or a downrange velocity

FIELD TOOLS
• Hit probability for a first-round hit from your group size, velocity SD, wind and range uncertainty
• Wind bracket on a slice of your reticle, with forecast gusts
• Range error: where the shot lands if the distance is off
• Range finding with the reticle (mil-dot ranging)

STEALTH MODE
• One tap stops every connection the app makes; location from GPS alone
• Quick Settings tile, and a warning while the phone's radios are on

SYNC AND BACKUP
• Optional sync between phones with your Google account
• Export and import all profiles as a file

Built for PRS, F-Class, ELR, long range hunting and target shooting with centerfire rifles. A calculator only predicts: always confirm your dope with live fire.
