Delete your account

Ballistics by KGW Labs (com.kgwlabs.ballistics)

An account exists only if you signed in with Google for cloud sync. Deleting it removes:

  • your profiles stored in the cloud (rifles, loads, settings),
  • your sign-in account (Google name, e-mail address and account ID).

Bullet suggestions you sent stay in the community database, no longer linked to your name or e-mail. Profiles on your phone are not touched; uninstall the app or clear its data to remove them.

In the app

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  2. In Sync, tap the Google button, then Delete account, and confirm.
  3. Pick your Google account once more to confirm it is you.

The account is deleted at once.

Without the app

Write to us, preferably from the Google account you signed in with, and ask for its deletion: kgwlabs@interia.com. We delete the account and its cloud data within 30 days and confirm by reply.
